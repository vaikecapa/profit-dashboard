ProfitApp OS privacy

ProfitApp OS is a private tool used only by the owners of ECOM Connect OÜ and CPA OÜ. It is not offered to anyone else.

When an owner connects Google Drive, the app reads and saves expense receipts in the two bookkeeping folders of those companies, so the bookkeeper finds them there. It does not open other files, does not share files, and does not sell or pass on any data. Receipt images are read by an AI model only to find the seller, sum and date.

Access can be removed at any time in the Google account under Security › Third-party apps.
